# Ant Colony – prototype (milestone 1)

Single-file HTML/Canvas game, portrait and touch-first. Play: https://2leggedanimal.github.io/ant-colony-prototype/

- **Ants** tool: tap an ant (or drag a box / *Select all*), then tap a tunnel to send them. Tap a 💀 body with ants selected to haul it to the pit, or press **Haul**.
- **Dig / Fill**: paint with your finger to carve or fill tunnels (grey rock can't be dug).
- 👑 Queen lays a new ant every 8 s (game time). Each ant lives 60–120 s (bar above it).
- **1x/4x** speed toggle.
